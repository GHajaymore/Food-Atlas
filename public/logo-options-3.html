I · The world's three ways of eating

food ✓✓ · wiki ✓✓ · free ✓ · global ✓✓

A spoon, a fork and a pair of chopsticks, radiating from a shared centre. It fixes exactly what is wrong with G — the arms differ, so it can never collapse into a compass — and it says something G could not: not one culture's table, but several at the same one. Still the collective, still nobody in the middle.
