## The headline: every record now has a path to Authentic

There was none. `assess()` picked a classification from the *shape* of the evidence —
heritage plus ingredients meant Authentic, ingredients plus an article meant Variation
— so a Variation stayed a Variation for ever. Community validation appeared in no
branch condition at all.

Promotion is now `score >= AUTHENTIC_AT (55)` **and** `validations >= VALIDATIONS_REQUIRED (3)`.

The threshold is safe by arithmetic rather than by rule. With no confirmations,
`localSource` and `community` are both zero, so published data alone tops out at:

```
(70 geography + 65 ingredients + 50 technique + 0 + 75 documentation + 0) / 6 = 43
```

43 < 55, so **no amount of documentation can promote a record**. A reader can add up
the six figures on the card and check the classification themselves, which is the
claim this atlas makes about all its numbers.

`VALIDATIONS_REQUIRED` is checked separately and the first draft missed it: a record
with a heritage designation *and* a register method reaches 58 on **one** confirmation,
so a single person could have authenticated a tradition on the strength of paperwork
that predated them. Three confirmations answers a different question — how many people
agree — and a score must not buy its way around it.

### Two errors found while building it

**Technique was credited to registers only, and that was backwards.** A dish with its
ingredients recorded and three confirmations from its own village scored 49 and stayed
Unverified, because no institution had written it down — which is exactly the dish this
atlas exists for. Someone from the place saying *this is how we make it* is the
**primary** evidence of traditional technique; a register documenting it is secondary. A
published account alone still counts for nothing.

**A confirmed record was told nobody had confirmed it.** The two "nothing is documented"
branches returned before validations were read, so a record with three of them displayed
*"nobody from the community has checked it"*.

### The ladder, verified end to end

| state | score | badge |
|---|---|---|
| bare record | — | Unverified |
| method + ingredients recorded | 23 | Unverified |
| + 1 person from the place confirms | 46 | Unverified |
| + 3 from the region | **58** | Authentic — Regional |
| + 3 from the town | **58** | Authentic — Local |

Local against regional is decided by *where the confirmations came from*, not by the
score. A higher number does not make a dish more local.

## What still blocks everything

Nothing writes `validations`, because the app has no writes at all. Both of these are
Ajay's to do and neither is a code problem:

1. **`EXPO_PUBLIC_CONTRIBUTION_FORM_URL`** — ten minutes with a Google Form. See
   `docs/contribution-form.md`, then `node scripts/setup-contribution-form.mjs "<link>"`
   which reads the six `entry.NNN` ids back out by matching marker words. It refuses to
   write when a required field is missing and merges into `.env` rather than replacing
   it.
2. **`EXPO_PUBLIC_CONFIRMATIONS_URL`** — needs the shared DB. Schema and two endpoints
   in `docs/confirmations-api.md`. This is the **only** part of the app that needs a
   server, and the reason is identity: a form cannot tell three people from one person
   submitting three times, and the entire badge rests on the unique index
   `(dish_id, person_id)`.

## Two bugs found by looking at the app, not the data

**The front page had stopped moving.** A shelf claimed only the twelve records it was
showing, so every later shelf's pool depended on what the earlier ones showed *that
day* — and that drifts as they rotate. The shelves above the cookable rail took 4 of its
records on day 0, 3 on day 1, 2 on day 2: the pool slid forward one record a day, which
is exactly what the rotation was adding, and the two cancelled. The rail sat frozen for
three days at a time while every part of the machinery looked correct. A shelf now
reserves its whole pool.

**Search ignored `localNames`.** The app displays a record in thirty-four languages and
could only be searched in the one it was filed under. It mattered most for the register
records, written in their own script — 龙井茶, ម្រេចកំពត — with the romanisation as an
alternate: all 237 were unreachable by anything a reader would type. "Kampot" now finds
ម្រេចកំពត.

## Identity

**Mark** — a gold disc with a spoon cut out, tilted 32°. Eleven marks were drawn across
four rounds; all are kept with their reasoning in `public/logo-options*.html`. **Read
those before redrawing anything.**

Three things that had to be measured rather than judged:

- Four identical spoons at 90° read as a **compass** at icon size, not as cutlery.
  Three *different* utensils cannot, so asymmetry was load-bearing.
- Three arms at 120° hang **10.75 units high** inside their box (four at 90° do not, so
  it appeared only on the swap).
- An upright disc-with-a-slot is a **keyhole**, which reads as *locked* — the opposite
  of the product. A keyhole is always vertical; the tilt kills it.

**Wordmark** — the mark lives inside the name: F**oo**dia, spoon in one o, fork in the
other. A mask, not a compound path: the bowl and stem overlap, and `evenodd` cancels
overlapping holes while `nonzero` gives a winding of −2 — either way a bright wedge
appears. `scripts/make-icons.mjs` renders the six `assets/` PNGs from the same numbers,
with no image dependency (coverage sampling + `node:zlib`).

**Tagline** — `Only local cooks can call it authentic.` Both words are the code:
`validatedLocally` is a real field, and `VALIDATIONS_REQUIRED` is 3, so one person never
authenticates anything. It states a *rule*, which is why it is honest — claiming an
achievement ("Confirmed by the people who cook it") would describe something that has
not happened. Every rejected candidate and its reason is in `brand.ts`.

## i18n: deliberately half-finished

`src/domain/uiLanguage.ts` (RFC 4647 negotiation, no new dependency) and `src/i18n/`
with twelve machine-translated catalogues that say so. Wired: NavRow, feed, search,
loading/error screens.

**Ajay's call, and it was the right one: finish extracting strings into the English
catalogue across all screens before translating anything else** — otherwise every new
key costs twelve retranslations. `translationCoverage()` reports what is left. Evidence
prose is excluded on purpose: a loose translation of "nobody has confirmed this"
misstates a record's standing.

## Corrections this repository had to make about itself

Both were written confidently and were false; both were caught by counting.

- **"Community validation is hardcoded 0 for all 18,008 records."** Zero for the 17,964
  that `assess()` scores. The other 44 carry their own breakdowns: 38 UNESCO
  inscriptions (credited 70 — an inscription *is* institutional community recognition)
  and 6 curated records assessed by hand (70–90).
- **"Confirmations are the only thing that can authenticate a record."** They are the
  only thing that can *promote* one. Heritage + ingredients classifies as
  Authentic — Regional by an older branch; 269 EU-register records reached that badge
  with nobody confirming anything.

This is the failure mode `types.ts` was bitten by before — a confident comment that
later makes correct code look wrong.

## Traps for next time

- **Metro does not notice a brand-new directory on Windows.** Adding `src/i18n/` gave
  `Unable to resolve ./copy` in the browser while `tsc` and `jest` both resolved it.
  Restart the dev server; it is not a code fault.
- **Backticks in `node -e` strings get executed by bash.** Bit twice this session. Use
  the Edit tool for prose containing backticks.
- **`rotation`/`originX`/`originY` on an SVG `G` leak to the DOM** in react-native-svg's
  web build and React logs an error on every paint. Use the `transform` string form.
- **Look at the rendered PNG, not the source.** All three logo faults were invisible in
  the SVG and obvious in the image.
